# Shark Mode Call Script

Keep it casual. Sound busy. Solve *their* problem.

## 1. Opening

**Seller:** "Yeah, I got your text about my house..."

**You:** "Hey! Yeah, I was out at the property (or looking at the data) earlier. I'm [Your Name]. Just to be clear—I'm a private buyer, not an agent looking for a listing. Did you get a chance to see that breakdown I sent over?"

## 2. Pain Probe

**You:** "Look, I buy a few of these a month. Before we talk numbers, just so I don't waste your time—**if I could close on the date of your choice and pay all the closing costs, what is the main reason you're looking to sell right now?**"

Listen for: inherited, tired of tenants, job move, divorce, repairs overwhelm.

## 3. Price Grumble

**Seller:** "Your offer is a bit low. Zillow says it's worth more."

**You:** "I hear you. Zillow doesn't see the [Roof/Kitchen/HVAC] in the estimate. My offer is **Cash, As-Is.** No inspections, no 6% agent commissions, no repairs. You walk away with that exact number. **How much would Zillow's 'value' actually be after you pay an agent $20k and spend $30k fixing it up?**"

## 4. Takeaway Close

**You:** "I've got two other properties I'm looking at in [Area] today, and I only have the capital to take on one more this month. If we can make the numbers work, I can get the paperwork to title this afternoon. **Does that price get us close enough to start escrow, or should I move on to the next deal?**"

Stop talking. First one to speak loses.

## After the call

Log the pain point + heat (Cold / Warm / Hot) in DealShark Notes.
